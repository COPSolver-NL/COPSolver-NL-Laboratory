# Beginning

Every scientific endeavor begins long before its first publication.

It begins with questions.

Some become hypotheses.

Others become research projects.

A few eventually evolve into research programs.

The COPSolver-NL Laboratory was established as a virtual research environment within GAMOS, dedicated to investigating how Artificial Intelligence can assist one of the most demanding stages of optimization research: understanding real-world problems before their mathematical formulation.

Rather than viewing optimization exclusively as the process of solving mathematical models, this laboratory investigates the intellectual process that precedes modeling itself: how knowledge expressed in natural language can be transformed into structured computational representations suitable for optimization and decision systems.

This perspective motivates the development of the COPSolver-NL postdoctoral research project and establishes the scientific foundations upon which broader research lines and, eventually, a long-term research program may naturally emerge.
