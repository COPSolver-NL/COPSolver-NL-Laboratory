# VISION

> *"A vision is not a prediction of the future. It is a commitment to help build it."*

---

# Our Vision

The **COPSolver-NL Laboratory** envisions a future in which mathematical modeling becomes more accessible, transparent, and scientifically rigorous through the integration of Natural Language, computational intelligence, and formal mathematical reasoning.

We believe that advances in Artificial Intelligence create an unprecedented opportunity to rethink how mathematical models are conceived, documented, validated, and communicated.

Our vision is not to replace mathematical reasoning, but to strengthen it by enabling more natural, transparent, and reproducible interactions between human knowledge and formal mathematical representations.

---

# A Scientific Vision

The laboratory seeks to contribute to a future where researchers can describe complex optimization and decision problems using Natural Language while preserving the rigor required for formal mathematical modeling.

We envision computational systems capable of assisting researchers throughout the modeling process by supporting interpretation, formalization, verification, documentation, and scientific reproducibility.

Such systems should always operate as scientific collaborators, promoting understanding rather than replacing scientific judgment.

---

# A Vision for Open Science

Scientific knowledge grows through openness, collaboration, and reproducibility.

For this reason, we envision a research environment in which scientific ideas, computational methods, mathematical models, software, and documentation evolve together as openly accessible scientific artifacts whenever possible.

Transparency is not merely a desirable characteristic of scientific software; it is a fundamental principle for trustworthy scientific research.

---

# A Vision for Education

We believe that future generations of scientists should be able to learn mathematical modeling through environments that encourage exploration, critical thinking, experimentation, and scientific rigor.

The laboratory therefore seeks to produce educational resources that help students, researchers, and practitioners understand not only how mathematical models are constructed, but also why they are constructed in particular ways.

Education is understood as an essential component of scientific progress.

---

# A Vision for Collaboration

The most significant scientific advances emerge from the combination of diverse perspectives.

The laboratory therefore promotes interdisciplinary collaboration among researchers in mathematics, computer science, optimization, artificial intelligence, operations research, engineering, and related fields.

We also recognize the growing role of computational systems in scientific research and encourage transparent human–AI collaboration whenever it contributes to scientific quality, intellectual honesty, and reproducibility.

---

# Looking Forward

The COPSolver-NL Laboratory is conceived as a long-term scientific initiative.

Its success will not be measured solely by software produced or publications completed, but by its contribution to advancing scientific knowledge, fostering collaboration, educating future researchers, and establishing reproducible scientific practices that benefit the broader research community.

We envision a laboratory whose greatest legacy is not simply the technology it develops, but the scientific principles it helps establish.
