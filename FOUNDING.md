# Founding Statement

The **COPSolver-NL Laboratory** was founded as an open scientific initiative dedicated to the study, development, and dissemination of mathematical and computational methods for understanding Natural Language and constructing formal mathematical models of Optimization and Decision Problems.

Rather than being created as a conventional software repository, the laboratory was conceived as a research environment where mathematical modeling, computational science, artificial intelligence, and open collaboration converge.

Its purpose is to transform scientific ideas into reproducible knowledge through rigorous research, transparent documentation, and open scientific collaboration.

---

# Mission

Our mission is to investigate how Natural Language can become a rigorous interface for the specification, interpretation, and formal mathematical modeling of optimization and decision problems.

We seek to bridge the gap between human reasoning and mathematical representation through transparent, reproducible, and scientifically grounded research.

---

# Vision

We envision a future in which optimization and decision problems can be described in Natural Language and systematically transformed into formal mathematical models without compromising mathematical rigor.

The laboratory aims to contribute not only software, but also scientific methodology, computational frameworks, educational resources, and reproducible research that can be extended by future generations of researchers.

---

# Principles

The laboratory is guided by the following principles:

- Scientific integrity above all.
- Mathematical rigor in every stage of research.
- Transparency of methods and scientific decisions.
- Reproducibility of research.
- Open science whenever ethically and legally possible.
- Respect for contributors and the scientific community.
- Continuous refinement through critical analysis, experimentation, and collaboration.

---

# Collaboration

The laboratory welcomes researchers, students, educators, and collaborators who share its scientific values.

Contributions are encouraged through scientific discussion, experimentation, documentation, software development, and collaborative research.

To preserve the coherence, quality, and integrity of the laboratory's scientific record, contributions to the official repositories are maintained through an appropriate review and governance process.

Open collaboration and responsible stewardship are complementary principles of this laboratory.

---

# Research Philosophy

Scientific knowledge evolves through observation, questioning, experimentation, and continuous refinement.

Ideas rarely emerge fully developed. They mature through experience, critical thinking, collaboration, and rigorous validation.

For this reason, the laboratory embraces the continuous evolution of its research while maintaining an uncompromising commitment to scientific rigor.

---

# The First Research Project

The first research project developed within this laboratory is:

## COPSolver-NL

A scientific initiative dedicated to investigating how Natural Language can support the construction of formal mathematical models for optimization and decision problems.

While COPSolver-NL is the laboratory's inaugural project, the laboratory itself is intended to support future scientific initiatives built upon the same principles.

---

# Open Science

Whenever possible, the laboratory adopts open-science practices.

Documentation, source code, mathematical formulations, computational artifacts, and research materials are intended to be publicly available whenever ethically, legally, and scientifically appropriate, allowing independent verification, reproduction, and extension of the research.

---

# Governance

The laboratory encourages broad participation from the scientific community.

Responsibility for maintaining the official scientific record, reviewing contributions, and preserving the long-term coherence of the laboratory remains with its maintainers.

This balance ensures both openness and scientific consistency.

---

# A Living Document

This document is intentionally conceived as a living document.

As the laboratory grows, new projects emerge, and new collaborators join, this founding statement may be refined while preserving its original purpose and principles.
