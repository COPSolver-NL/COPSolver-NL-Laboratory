# COPSolver-NL Laboratory

> **COPSolver-NL Laboratory is an open scientific research environment dedicated to rigorous methodology, transparent documentation, and collaborative Human–AI knowledge construction. The laboratory was conceived to support the development of scientific research while simultaneously documenting and critically examining the methodologies through which that research is produced.**

---

## Welcome

Welcome to the institutional repository of the **COPSolver-NL Laboratory**.

This repository documents the laboratory's scientific development, governance, institutional policies, research documentation, and public scientific records. It provides a transparent and continuously evolving record of the laboratory's activities.

---

## Research Environment

| Environment | Role |
|--------------|------|
| **Physical Research Environment** | IPRJ – Postdoctoral Research |
| **Dynamic Digital Research Environment** | ChatGPT – Collaborative Knowledge Construction |
| **Institutional Repository and Permanent Archive** | GitHub – Documentation, Governance, Version History, and Public Releases |

---

## Repository Structure

This repository is organized into dedicated institutional and research directories.

Each document has a single primary purpose and complements the others, ensuring clarity, traceability, consistency, and long-term maintainability.

---

## Documentation Policy

The laboratory prioritizes clarity, rigor, consistency, and transparent documentation throughout the research process.
